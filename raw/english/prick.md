## 语境

There are bubbles and then the things that **prick** the bubbles, okay? The things that prick the bubbles, typically in the beginning, are something that means that I have to sell some wealth to get money.

> 有泡沫，就有刺破泡沫的东西。最初刺破泡沫的，通常是那些迫使我不得不变卖财富换取现金的事情。
