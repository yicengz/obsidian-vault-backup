## 语境

Classic strong hands is that when weak investors, not knowledgeable investors, then put a lot of money into it, particularly if that's in a **leveraged** way.

> 典型的"弱手"信号是：不懂行的弱势投资者把大量资金投入其中，尤其是以加杠杆的方式。
