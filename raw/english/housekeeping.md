> I'm going to be talking to you and doing various bits of podcast **housekeeping**. I've got just some stuff that I need to deal with, stuff I need to manage.
> 我要跟你们聊聊，顺便处理一些播客的**日常事务**。就是一些我得交代、得处理的事儿。

搭配：
- a bit of housekeeping before we start — 开始之前先说几件事
- household housekeeping — 家务
- housekeeping tasks — 打扫/整理类事务

记忆点：英美会议、播客、演讲一开场经常说 "Just a bit of housekeeping first…"，指"正题之前先交代几件小事"。
