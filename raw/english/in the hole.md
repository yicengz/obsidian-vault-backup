## 语境

So I owe the bank $50, but now this thing that I have that was worth $100 a couple of months ago is now worth $25 and I'm $25 **in the hole**.

> 我欠银行 50 美元，可手里这个几个月前还值 100 美元的东西现在只值 25 美元了——我等于亏空了 25 美元。
