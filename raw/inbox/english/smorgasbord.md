> A long thing with like a sort of best of the podcast where you hear from all my regular guests in some format, some incredible **smorgasbord** of guest appearances. What's a **smorgasbord** is the question from my daughter. It's basically a kind of a large plate or tray covered in different types of food.
> 一集很长的合集节目，让所有常驻嘉宾都以某种形式出现——一场超级豪华的**嘉宾大杂烩**。我女儿问 smorgasbord 是啥？基本就是那种一大盘/一大托盘上摆满各种食物的东西。

搭配：
- a smorgasbord of options — 一大堆可选项
- a smorgasbord of talent — 各路人才荟萃
- a smorgasbord of flavours — 百味杂陈

发音提示：/ˈsmɔːɡəsˌbɔːd/（英）或 /ˈsmɔːrɡəsˌbɔːrd/（美），第一个 s 发轻音，不是"smor-gas-board"。

词源：来自瑞典语 *smörgåsbord*——smörgås（三明治）+ bord（桌子）。文化背景是北欧的自助大餐。
