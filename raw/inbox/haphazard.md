> I'll attempt to give you the same number of episodes I would normally give you during this period, but they just, it might be a bit **haphazard**. My uploading schedule might be a little bit **haphazard**.
> 我尽量保持平常那个更新数量，但可能会有点**没规律**。上线时间可能会比较**杂乱**。

搭配：
- a haphazard approach — 一种随意的做法
- in a haphazard way / manner — 杂乱无章地
- haphazard arrangement — 无序的安排

对比记忆：
- **haphazard**（形容词，强调"无计划")
- **random**（更中性，只说"随机")
- **chaotic**（更强，接近"混乱")
