SpaceX ([SPCX](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/SPCX#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "Space Exploration Technologies Corp.")) [disclosed](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2066873915717136548?s=20 "disclosed") this morning that it is [officially acquiring](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4603904-spacex-to-acquire-ai-startup-cursor-in-60b-deal#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "officially acquiring") artificial intelligence startup Anysphere, the maker of Cursor, for $60B in stock.

The disclosure follows the curious [announcement](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4577587-musks-spacex-weighs-60b-buyout-of-cursor-or-10b-partnership-payout#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "announcement") in April, which gave SpaceX the option to acquire Cursor for $60B or pay it $10B for its work.

Given the interest in Cursor from SpaceX and other investors (it had funding discussions as late [as April](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4576403-ai-startup-cursor-said-to-be-in-talks-to-raise-2b-funding-round-at-valuation-of-over-50b#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "as April")), many are asking: what is Cursor?

## What Cursor does

> ## Cursor 是做什么的

Cursor creates [some](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4570931-jfrog-jumps-as-its-platform-lands-on-cursor-marketplace#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "some") of the leading AI coding agents; it's used by industry leaders such as Nvidia ([NVDA](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/NVDA#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "NVIDIA Corporation")), Adobe ([ADBE](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/ADBE#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "Adobe Inc.")), Figma ([FIG](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/FIG#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "Figma, Inc.")), and OpenAI ([OPENAI](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/OPENAI#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "OpenAI")). More than 1M developers use its products.

More than half of its revenue is believed to come from corporate customers, both from companies using Cursor for the first time and existing customers adding more seats. Software programmers at several companies have used its products.

> 据信其超过一半的营收来自企业客户——既包括首次接触 Cursor 的公司，也包括追加坐席（seat）的存量客户。多家公司的软件工程师都在使用其产品。

In the AI coding agent space, it competes with OpenAI ([OPENAI](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/OPENAI#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "OpenAI")), Anthropic ([ANTHRO](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/ANTHRO#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "Anthropic")), and Google ([GOOG](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/GOOG#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "Alphabet Inc.")) ([GOOGL](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/GOOGL#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "Alphabet Inc.")), among others.

> 在 AI 编程 agent 这一赛道，它的竞争对手包括 OpenAI（[OPENAI](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/OPENAI#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "OpenAI")）、Anthropic（[ANTHRO](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/ANTHRO#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "Anthropic")）以及谷歌（[GOOG](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/GOOG#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "Alphabet Inc.") / [GOOGL](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/GOOGL#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "Alphabet Inc.")）等。

## Why does SpaceX want Cursor?

> ## SpaceX 为什么想要 Cursor？

The deal significantly expands Elon Musk's tech empire into enterprise AI, extending a post-IPO rally that has already pushed the company's market cap past $2.8 trillion.

> 这笔交易将马斯克的科技帝国大幅扩展至企业级 AI 领域，也延续了 SpaceX 上市后的涨势——目前其市值已突破 2.8 万亿美元。

In November 2025, Cursor raised about $2.3B at a $29.3B post-money valuation in a Series D funding round, which included existing investors, including Accel, Thrive, and Andreessen Horowitz, and new partners Coatue, Nvidia ([NVDA](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/NVDA#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "NVIDIA Corporation")), and Alphabet's Google.

> 2025 年 11 月，Cursor 在 D 轮融资中以 293 亿美元投后估值募集了约 23 亿美元，参与者包括老股东 Accel、Thrive、Andreessen Horowitz，以及新进入的 Coatue、英伟达（[NVDA](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/NVDA#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "NVIDIA Corporation")）和 Alphabet 旗下的谷歌。

In March, Cursor [topped](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4560015-ai-coding-firm-cursor-reaches-2b-annual-revenue-rate#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "topped") $2B in annual revenue run rate, showcasing explosive growth from its corporate customers.

Musk [hinted](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4577587-musks-spacex-weighs-60b-buyout-of-cursor-or-10b-partnership-payout#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "hinted") in April that the combination of Cursor and SpaceX's access to the Colossus data center would allow it to better compete in the artificial intelligence model space.

“The combination of Cursor’s leading product and distribution to expert software engineers with SpaceX’s million H100 equivalent Colossus training supercomputer will allow us to build the world’s most useful models,” SpaceX said in a post on X. “SpaceXAI and @cursor_ai are now working closely together to create the world’s best coding and knowledge work AI.”

Goldman Sachs [recently forecast](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4600816-spacex-ai-related-revenue-expected-to-jump-100-times-by-2030-report#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link%7Cfirst_level_url%3Anews "recently forecast") that SpaceX's AI-related revenue would jump 100 times by 2030, no doubt aided partly because of Cursor.
