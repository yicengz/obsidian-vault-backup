## Nvidia’s Evolution to a Potential Dividend Aristocrat
## 英伟达向潜在股息贵族的进化

The fear of missing out ("FOMO") is a relatively new adage for a generationally old phenomenon. And this anxiety has been no stranger to investors who are constantly looking for the next great company -consider the recent IPO [euphoria](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4913914-spacex-trillion-dollar-moonshot-3-aerospace-defense-stocks-delivering-50-percent-plus-eps-growth#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/article/4913914-spacex-trillion-dollar-moonshot-3-aerospace-defense-stocks-delivering-50-percent-plus-eps-growth") surrounding SpaceX ([SPCX](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/SPCX#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Space Exploration Technologies Corp.")).
「错失恐惧症」（FOMO）是一个相对较新的说法，但它描述的现象却古已有之。对于那些始终在寻找下一家伟大公司的投资者而言，这种焦虑并不陌生——想想最近围绕 SpaceX（[SPCX](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/SPCX#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Space Exploration Technologies Corp.")) 的 IPO [狂热](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4913914-spacex-trillion-dollar-moonshot-3-aerospace-defense-stocks-delivering-50-percent-plus-eps-growth#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/article/4913914-spacex-trillion-dollar-moonshot-3-aerospace-defense-stocks-delivering-50-percent-plus-eps-growth") 就知道了。

Humans are emotionally attached to success, especially in investing. Staring at a stock chart that has gone vertical as you sit shareless is an agonizing investing experience, especially when looking back at Nvidia's ([NVDA](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/NVDA#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "NVIDIA Corporation")) meteoric rise over the past decade.
人类在情感上容易被成功所吸引，尤其是在投资中。当你空仓时，却盯着一根垂直拉升的股价走势图，这是一种极其痛苦的投资体验——尤其是在回顾英伟达（[NVDA](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/NVDA#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "NVIDIA Corporation")) 过去十年间流星般的上涨时。

Conventional wisdom would have you believe that the explosive growth phase for any company is fundamentally complete once it reaches market-topping valuation levels. History shows that investors felt anxiety during previous generational-defining technological booms. In the late 1990s and early 2000s, many investors believed that the profit-making windows for the emergent Internet and computing stalwarts had closed. What they didn't realize was that what they were looking at was the maturation of shareholder return.
传统观念会让你相信，一旦一家公司达到市场顶尖的估值水平，其爆发式增长阶段就基本结束了。历史表明，投资者在此前那些定义一代人的科技繁荣时期也曾感到焦虑。在上世纪 90 年代末和本世纪初，许多投资者认为新兴的互联网和计算巨头已经错过了盈利窗口。他们没有意识到的是，他们看到的其实是股东回报机制的成熟。

## Nvidia's Dividend Declaration
## 英伟达的股息公告

On [May 20, 2026](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4595531-nvidia-raises-dividend-to-025-announces-80b-buyback#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/news/4595531-nvidia-raises-dividend-to-025-announces-80b-buyback"), Nvidia's capital allocation strategy quietly signaled a shift when it raised its quarterly payout from a nominal penny to $0.25 per share ($1.00 annually). However, Nvidia has seen such a monumental rise in its valuation that this 2,400% dividend increase registers a meager ~0.50% forward dividend yield. What might appear to be a dismissive level of income could actually be just the start of a new long-term capital plan for Nvidia.
在 [2026 年 5 月 20 日](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4595531-nvidia-raises-dividend-to-025-announces-80b-buyback#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/news/4595531-nvidia-raises-dividend-to-025-announces-80b-buyback")，英伟达的资本配置策略悄然转变：它将季度派息从象征性的 1 美分提高至每股 0.25 美元（年化 1 美元）。然而，英伟达的估值已经上涨了如此之多，以至于这次 2,400% 的股息增幅只带来了约 0.50% 的远期股息率。这个看似微不足道的收益水平，实际上可能只是英伟达全新长期资本计划的开端。

## Can Nvidia Follow IBM’s Dividend Playbook?
## 英伟达能效仿 IBM 的股息剧本吗？

To understand how lucrative this second act can be for "late" investors, look at data from the tech giants that have previously completed this same pivot. While IBM is the only true dividend aristocrat in the table below, Nvidia is a dividend ascendant like Texas Instruments ([TXN](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/TXN#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Texas Instruments Incorporated")), Microsoft ([MSFT](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/MSFT#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Microsoft Corporation")), and Apple ([AAPL](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/AAPL#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Apple Inc.")), showcasing strong free cash flow profiles that deliver value to shareholders through steady dividend growth.
要理解这「第二幕」对「后知后觉」的投资者来说可以多么有利可图，不妨看看那些曾经完成同样转型的科技巨头的数据。虽然下表中 IBM 是唯一的真正股息贵族，但英伟达正像德州仪器（[TXN](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/TXN#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Texas Instruments Incorporated"))、微软（[MSFT](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/MSFT#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Microsoft Corporation")) 和苹果（[AAPL](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/AAPL#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Apple Inc.")) 一样，是一只正在崛起的股息新秀，展现出强劲的自由现金流特征，并通过稳定的股息增长为股东创造价值。

Nvidia mirrors the likes of Microsoft and Apple, which were once considered purely growth stocks but matured into dividend-paying growth companies. The AI frenzy and infrastructure spending may be creating a new elite class of dividend leaders - a next dividend dynasty, with Nvidia on the path from hyper-growth titan to potential income champion, similar to IBM's start.
英伟达与微软和苹果颇为相似——这些公司曾被视为纯粹的成长股，但后来成熟为派息型成长公司。AI 狂热和基础设施支出可能正在催生一个新的股息领袖精英阶层，即「下一个股息王朝」，而英伟达正沿着从超高速增长巨头到潜在收益冠军的道路前进，类似于 IBM 当年的起点。

**IBM ([IBM](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/IBM#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "International Business Machines Corporation")):** After dominating early computing, IBM went through a highly volatile corporate evolution in the early 1990s before executing a massive structural turnaround. In 1995, it launched its modern, unbroken streak of dividend increases. Investors who thought IBM was an obsolete tech relic missed a massive second act: IBM is a certified Dividend Aristocrat with 31 consecutive years of dividend hikes, turning a modest post-turnaround position into a compounding giant that delivered a ~2,200% total return (~10.6% annualized) with dividends reinvested.
**IBM（[IBM](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/IBM#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "International Business Machines Corporation"))：** 在主导了早期计算行业之后，IBM 在 1990 年代初经历了极为动荡的企业演变，随后进行了大规模结构性转型。1995 年，IBM 开启了现代意义上连续不间断的股息增长纪录。那些认为 IBM 是过时科技遗物的投资者，错过了一场宏大的「第二幕」：IBM 是认证的股息贵族，已连续 31 年提高股息，将转型后一个不起眼的仓位变成了复利巨人，股息再投资后的总回报约为 2,200%（年化约 10.6%）。

While the road to aristocracy is long, Nvidia's profile - like IBM's - looks increasingly attractive as a dividend compounder. Admittedly, none of these companies will likely satisfy high-yield chasers, but part of that is because they're still experiencing price appreciation. The bigger picture here is that these companies offer growth paired with some reliable income. In today's highly volatile market landscape, AI tailwinds and technological innovation allow companies to forge ahead, capturing both [growth and income](https://seekingalpha.com/quant-growth-income/picks/current#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/quant-growth-income/picks/current"), and are becoming extremely valuable.
尽管成为股息贵族的道路漫长，但英伟达——与 IBM 一样——作为一只股息复利机器，其形象正变得越来越有吸引力。诚然，这些公司都不太可能满足追求高股息率的投资者，但部分原因在于它们的股价仍在上涨。更宏观的图景是，这些公司提供了增长与相对可靠收益的组合。在当今高度波动的市场环境中，AI 东风和技术创新使这些公司能够勇往直前，同时捕捉 [增长与收益](https://seekingalpha.com/quant-growth-income/picks/current#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/quant-growth-income/picks/current")，并变得极具价值。

The Quant Case for Nvidia (momentum, strong earnings and cash flow, profitability) could be the building blocks for dividend greatness. Far from being a growth story that has already run its course, Nvidia is establishing itself to potentially become the next great dividend aristocrat.
英伟达的量化投资逻辑（动量强劲、盈利与现金流充沛、盈利能力突出）可能成为其成就伟大股息公司的基石。英伟达远非一个已经讲完的成长故事，它正在确立自身地位，有望成为下一个伟大的股息贵族。
