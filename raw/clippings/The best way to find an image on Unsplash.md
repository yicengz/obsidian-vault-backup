With millions of images on Unsplash, the hardest part isn’t whether the image exists; it’s knowing how to find it.

The good news: a few simple search techniques can dramatically improve your results. Whether you’re looking for something highly specific or just browsing for inspiration, here are 6 tips on how to get the most out of Unsplash search.

---

## 1\. Start with the right search

How you write your search can completely change the results you get.

### If you know exactly what you want:

If you have something very specific in mind, the best approach is to **describe it clearly using multiple keywords**.

Instead of searching something broad like:  
*“office”*

Try writing what you actually mean:

- “woman working on laptop in cafe”
- “minimal modern living room with plants”
- “golden retriever puppy running in snow”
<video src="https://storage.ghost.io/c/80/1d/801d5d13-5875-4136-9bfc-1e2fe01b2bff/content/media/2026/03/1.2-1.mp4" width="1610" height="1080" controls=""></video>0:05

Unsplash search has improved to better understand natural language queries, so writing a more detailed description can often surface exactly what you’re looking for.

### If you’re browsing for inspiration:

Go broader. Start with **one or two keywords** and see what comes up.

Searches like “ *spring* ”, “ *coffee* ”, and “ *workspace* ” will show a wide range of images, styles, and compositions.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/80/1d/801d5d13-5875-4136-9bfc-1e2fe01b2bff/content/images/2026/03/2.4.gif)

This will show a wider range of styles and compositions, helping you discover the direction or visual mood you want before refining your search.

## 2\. Photos or Illustrations

Choose **Photos** for real-world scenes and authentic moments, or **Illustrations** for clean, graphic visuals used in presentations, product design, and marketing. You can switch between the two mediums right under the search bar.

## 3\. Refine your results with filters

Once you’ve started a search, filters help you quickly narrow things down.

### License Filter

Filter by the type of content you want to see:

- **Free** – images from the Unsplash open library
- [**Unsplash+**](https://unsplash.com/plus) – exclusive content for subscribers
- **All** – the default view
![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/80/1d/801d5d13-5875-4136-9bfc-1e2fe01b2bff/content/images/2026/03/5-3.gif)

### Orientation Filter

Choose the layout that fits your project:

- **Landscape** – great for websites and presentations
- **Portrait** – ideal for mobile layouts and social posts
- **Square** – useful for thumbnails and social graphics
![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/80/1d/801d5d13-5875-4136-9bfc-1e2fe01b2bff/content/images/2026/03/5.1-2.gif)

### Illustration Style Filters

If you’re searching for vector illustrations, you can also refine results by style.

Available illustration styles include:

- **Flat** – clean, minimal graphics with simple shapes and colors
- **3D** – dimensional illustrations with depth and lighting
- **Hand-drawn** – organic, sketch-style visuals with a human feel
- **Line Art** – minimal outlines and simplified forms
![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/80/1d/801d5d13-5875-4136-9bfc-1e2fe01b2bff/content/images/2026/03/6-6.gif)

These filters make it much easier to quickly find illustrations that match the **visual style of your project or brand.**

## 4\. Browse Topics

If you’re not sure what to search for, **Topics** are a great starting point. Topics are curated daily by the Unsplash team and organize images around popular subjects like:

- [3D Renders](https://unsplash.com/t/3d-renders)
- [Nature](https://unsplash.com/t/nature)
- [Film](https://unsplash.com/t/film)
- [Architecture](https://unsplash.com/t/architecture-interior)
- [People](https://unsplash.com/t/people)
![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/80/1d/801d5d13-5875-4136-9bfc-1e2fe01b2bff/content/images/2026/03/4.1-1.gif)

Instead of starting from a blank search, Topics give you a **curated way to explore some of the most popular visual categories on Unsplash.**

## 5\. Use one image to discover more

Often, the fastest way to find the right image is to first find the **right vibe**. When you click on an image on Unsplash, you’ll see additional suggestions underneath it.

### Related Images

Unsplash will show **visually similar images** that share the same subject and composition. If you like an image, this is one of the quickest ways to explore variations.

<video src="https://storage.ghost.io/c/80/1d/801d5d13-5875-4136-9bfc-1e2fe01b2bff/content/media/2026/03/7.mp4" width="1610" height="1080" controls=""></video>0:00

### From this Series

For Unsplash+ images, you may also see **“From this series.”**

These are photos from the same shoot, meaning the lighting, setting, and aesthetic all match.

<video src="https://storage.ghost.io/c/80/1d/801d5d13-5875-4136-9bfc-1e2fe01b2bff/content/media/2026/03/8.1.mp4" width="1610" height="1080" controls=""></video>0:00

/0:15

This is incredibly useful when you need multiple cohesive images for things like websites, marketing campaigns, or presentations. Think of it as finding **a full visual set**, not just a single image.

---

## 6\. Search by image

Already have an image you like? Let it do the searching for you.

With Visual Search, you simply click the Visual Search icon in the search bar and drop the image or URL into the pop-up to search for similar visual results.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/80/1d/801d5d13-5875-4136-9bfc-1e2fe01b2bff/content/images/2026/03/Visual-Search--Twitter--2.jpg)

This is especially useful when:

- You’re trying to match a specific style, color palette, or composition
- You’ve seen an image somewhere and want something similar
- You want to find the original Unsplash image or photographer

Think of it as a shortcut to the exact vibe you’re after, without having to describe it.

---

## Search smarter, not longer

The best Unsplash searches usually follow a simple flow:

Start broad or specific depending on what you need → find an image that feels right → refine with filters → explore related results or the rest of the series.

A few small search habits can make a huge difference, and help you quickly land on exactly what you need… or something even better.
