The truth is, for all the noise surrounding SpaceX, formally known as Space Exploration Technologies Corp., the stock has been pretty quiet. The shares were priced at $135 in the biggest initial public offering ever on June 11, and they opened for trading at $150 on June 12. The IPO captivated investors as the stock soared in its first few sessions. But it quickly came tumbling back down, closing Thursday at $152.16 after dropping below $150 for a couple of sessions.

事实是，尽管围绕 SpaceX——正式名称为太空探索技术公司——的喧嚣不断，其股价却相当平静。该公司股票在 6 月 11 日有史以来最大规模的首次公开募股中定价为 135 美元，并于 6 月 12 日开盘交易价为 150 美元。IPO 吸引了投资者，股价在最初几个交易日飙升。但很快又跌回原形，周四收于 152.16 美元，此前曾有几个交易日跌破 150 美元。

Tesla shares are also up slightly since the IPO. They opened on June 12 at $399.49, closed that day at $406.43, and now are at $406.55. Meanwhile, traders are actively debating if, when and how SpaceX and Tesla will merge to create one gigantic Musk conglomerate. Indeed, the speculation of some form of combination appears to be preventing the initial concerns about Tesla's "Musk premium" from hitting its stock price.

自 IPO 以来，特斯拉股价也略有上涨。6 月 12 日开盘价为 399.49 美元，当日收于 406.43 美元，目前为 406.55 美元。与此同时，交易员们正积极争论 SpaceX 和特斯拉是否、何时以及如何合并，以打造一个庞大的马斯克企业集团。事实上，某种形式合并的猜测似乎正在阻止最初关于特斯拉“马斯克溢价”受到冲击的担忧影响其股价。

"SpaceX is a phenomenon strictly because of the Elon Musk halo," said Steve Sosnick, chief strategist at Interactive Brokers. "I have long referred to Tesla as a 'faith-based' stock, and that faith is well-earned after Musk made huge money for many of his most dedicated believers."

“SpaceX 之所以成为现象级公司，完全是因为埃隆·马斯克的光环，”盈透证券首席策略师史蒂夫·索斯尼克表示。“我一直把特斯拉称为‘基于信仰’的股票，而在马斯克为许多最忠实的信徒赚取了巨额财富之后，这种信仰是实至名归的。”

History says investors shouldn't be too worried about SpaceX's muted showing in the market so far. After all, it's what happened to Tesla following its IPO 16 years ago. The shares opened for trading at $1.27 on June 29, 2010, and closed that day at $1.59. A month later were trading at around $1.35. They're up almost 30,000% since then.

历史告诉我们，投资者不必过于担心 SpaceX 迄今为止在市场上的平淡表现。毕竟，16 年前特斯拉 IPO 后也是这样的。2010 年 6 月 29 日，特斯拉股票开盘价为 1.27 美元，当日收于 1.59 美元。一个月后股价约在 1.35 美元左右交易。自那以后，涨幅已接近 30,000%。

Investors hope Musk can repeat or exceed that performance with SpaceX, which was founded more than two decades ago and waited much longer to go public than Tesla did. Though it's currently unprofitable, the company has a $2 trillion market capitalization, which is higher than Tesla's $1.5 trillion. It would be sixth most valuable company in the S&P 500 if it was a member of the index.

投资者希望马斯克能在 SpaceX 上重复或超越这一表现。SpaceX 成立于 20 多年前，比特斯拉等待上市的时间长得多。尽管目前尚未盈利，但该公司市值已达 2 万亿美元，高于特斯拉的 1.5 万亿美元。如果它是标普 500 指数成分股，将位列第六大最有价值公司。

Retail traders are still buying SpaceX's stock, which has yet to see a day of net negative flows, according to Vanda Research data as of Wednesday. And Wall Street is unflinchingly bullish on the shares. Of the 35 analysts tracked by Bloomberg who follow the company, 29 have buy ratings. The average price target of $236 implies a 55% gain over the next 12 months.

根据 Vanda Research 截至周三的数据，散户交易者仍在买入 SpaceX 股票，该股尚未出现单日资金净流出。华尔街对这只股票也毫不迟疑地看好。在彭博社追踪的关注该公司的 35 位分析师中，29 位给予“买入”评级。平均目标价 236 美元意味着未来 12 个月有 55% 的上涨空间。

	## Why did SpaceX's stock fizzle out?

	## SpaceX 的股票为什么熄火了？

	At its peak, SpaceX was trading at 142 times its 2025 revenue of $18.7 billion. Even after its pullback, it's still trading at 106 times last year's sales. That's a bubbly price-to-sales ratio for a company that grew its revenue by 33% in 2025. It's also unprofitable, as losses from its space division and xAI (acquired earlier this year) are wiping out Starlink's profits.

	在巅峰时期，SpaceX 股价对应 2025 年 187 亿美元收入的 142 倍。即使回调后，仍以去年销售额的 106 倍交易。对于一家 2025 年收入增长 33% 的公司来说，这是一个泡沫化的市销率。公司也未盈利，其航天部门以及今年早些时候收购的 xAI 所造成的亏损正在抹杀星链的利润。

	Elon Musk claims SpaceX's revenue could surpass $1 trillion by 2030, but we'll need to see how it fares over the next few quarters before putting too much stock in the ambitious outlook. So for now, I'd avoid SpaceX and accumulate some more Bitcoin instead.

	埃隆·马斯克声称 SpaceX 的收入到 2030 年可能超过 1 万亿美元，但在这个雄心勃勃的前景上押注太多之前，我们还需要看看未来几个季度的表现如何。所以目前，我会避开 SpaceX，转而增持一些比特币。

	## SpaceX 史无前例，但其 IPO 却在步前辈后尘

	SpaceX has a very unusual business. It's an eccentric mash-up of recent advances in technology, from [artificial intelligence](https://www.fool.com/investing/stock-market/market-sectors/information-technology/ai-stocks/?utm_source=yahoo-host-full&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=article&referring_guid=28340d6c-2417-4e65-819f-52e9f37db66b) (AI) to satellite deployment to space-based connectivity. That's not to say it lacks a core purpose -- it wants to make humanity "multiplanetary" -- but its businesses differentiate it from most young companies, which focus on one or two things before expanding into something new.

	SpaceX 的业务非常不寻常。它是近期技术进步的大杂烩，从[人工智能](https://www.fool.com/investing/stock-market/market-sectors/information-technology/ai-stocks/?utm_source=yahoo-host-full&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=article&referring_guid=28340d6c-2417-4e65-819f-52e9f37db66b)（AI）到卫星部署，再到天基连接。这并不是说它缺乏核心目标——它希望让人类成为“多行星物种”——但其业务使其与大多数年轻公司不同，后者通常先专注于一两件事，然后再扩展到新领域。

	SpaceX is generating billions in revenue, with its Starlink internet services raking in most of that revenue. On its own, that part of the business pulled off a $4.4 billion operating profit in 2025, despite the full business posting a net loss of about $5 billion. Although the company is not profitable yet, it thinks its total addressable market is $28.5 trillion -- a massive opportunity, if the figure is accurate.

	SpaceX 正在创造数十亿美元收入，其星链互联网服务贡献了大部分收入。仅这一部分业务就在 2025 年实现了 44 亿美元的营业利润，尽管整个公司净亏损约 50 亿美元。虽然公司尚未盈利，但它认为其总可寻址市场为 28.5 万亿美元——如果这一数字准确，将是一个巨大的机遇。

	That $28.5 trillion revenue estimate was published in its IPO filing in late May, and by mid-June, excitement had reached a feverish pitch. As with many IPOs with massive capital raises, however, that enthusiasm has all but fizzled out now.

	这一 28.5 万亿美元的收入预期发布于其 5 月底的 IPO 招股书中，到 6 月中旬，兴奋情绪已达到狂热顶点。然而，与许多大规模募资的 IPO 一样，这种热情现在几乎已经消退殆尽。

	That leads us to this: Most IPO stocks have aggressive first days, but their first year on the market can be underwhelming. Take **Meta Platforms**, formerly Facebook. The stock's debut was a cultural phenomenon; its first year on the market was a deflating 32% loss.

	这引出了以下结论：大多数 IPO 股票首日表现激进，但上市第一年可能令人失望。以**Meta Platforms**，前身为 Facebook，为例。该股首次亮相是一个文化现象；但其上市第一年却令人沮丧地下跌了 32%。
