## U.S. Production Grows Despite Uncertainty

The U.S. manufacturing sector in July expanded at its fastest rate in more than four years, with solid demand seen across aerospace and defense, machinery, electronics, and activities tied to the AI infrastructure buildout, according to the latest [ISM Manufacturing PMI](https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-pmi-reports/pmi/july/ "https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-pmi-reports/pmi/july/"). Reaching its highest reading since May 2022, the index posted its seventh consecutive month of growth, led by increases in key demand signals including production, new export orders, backlogs, and employment.

[![ISM Manufacturing PMI grows](https://static.seekingalpha.com/uploads/2026/8/5/50113560-17859479724223056_origin.png?io=w640)](https://static.seekingalpha.com/uploads/2026/8/5/50113560-17859479724223056_origin.png)

ISM

Meanwhile, the macro backdrop continues to improve, with the Atlanta Fed's [GDPNow model](https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow/current-and-past-gdpnow-commentaries "https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow/current-and-past-gdpnow-commentaries") projecting the economy to grow by roughly 6% in Q3 2026, driven by resilient investment and consumer demand. Moreover, last month, the [Fed reported](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260729a.htm "https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260729a.htm") that productivity and capital spending have been strong. The solid economic activity has come despite uncertainty stemming from the Iran conflict, sticky inflation, and rising energy prices.

As manufacturing activity advances, the S&P 500 industrial sector (XLI) has been the index's third [highest-performing](https://seekingalpha.com/etfs-and-funds/etf-tables/sectors#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/etfs-and-funds/etf-tables/sectors") sector in 2026, up about 18% year-to-date. Many [hot industrial stocks](https://seekingalpha.com/screeners/96793112-Top-Industrial-Stocks#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/screeners/96793112-Top-Industrial-Stocks") have been boosted by demand stemming from the AI boom and big defense budgets.

Key drivers of U.S. industrial growth, based on my analysis, include AI data center-related construction, power systems, and [record defense spending](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4617152-house-narrowly-passes-115t-defense-bill#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/news/4617152-house-narrowly-passes-115t-defense-bill").
