## Dow Tops Record High

From its first close at 40.94 in 1896 to [recent highs](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4603674-dow-tops-record-high-and-heres-how-all-30-stocks-rank-according-to-sa-quant-ratings#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/news/4603674-dow-tops-record-high-and-heres-how-all-30-stocks-rank-according-to-sa-quant-ratings") above 51,000, the Dow Jones Industrial Average Index ([DJI](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/DJI#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Dow Jones Industrial Average Index")) is the [oldest continuous barometer](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-dow-jones-industrial-average-etf-trust-dia "https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-dow-jones-industrial-average-etf-trust-dia") of the U.S. stock market, containing some of the world's most recognizable brand names. The Dow hit a record high this week amid optimism over a long-awaited U.S.-Iran peace agreement and improving [consumer sentiment](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-12/us-consumer-sentiment-picks-up-on-easing-gasoline-prices?utm_source=augurdigest.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=augur-digest-262&_bhlid=6c7bfe0d30a68b43fa6f33312d4466ed342f4a17 "https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-12/us-consumer-sentiment-picks-up-on-easing-gasoline-prices?utm_source=augurdigest.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=augur-digest-262&_bhlid=6c7bfe0d30a68b43fa6f33312d4466ed342f4a17"), while Treasury yields moved lower.

[![Michigan consumer sentiment](https://static.seekingalpha.com/uploads/2026/6/15/50113560-17815574749446278_origin.png?io=w640)](https://static.seekingalpha.com/uploads/2026/6/15/50113560-17815574749446278_origin.png)

Augur Infinity

The University of Michigan's consumer sentiment indicator rose in early June for the first time in four months as lower gas prices provided relief amid stubborn inflation. Meanwhile, the [Atlanta Fed's model](https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow/current-and-past-gdpnow-commentaries "https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow/current-and-past-gdpnow-commentaries") is projecting real GDP to rise by 3.3% in the second quarter, up from an estimate of 3.0% earlier this month. Industrial production has [gradually improved](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/current/default.htm "https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/current/default.htm") in the past three months, lifted by business investment.

A price-weighted index tracking 30 blue-chip companies, the Dow has differentiated itself through a [curated approach](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/methodologies/methodology-dj-averages.pdf "https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/methodologies/methodology-dj-averages.pdf") that emphasizes corporate reputation and broad representation of the U.S. economy. It remains one of the most closely watched benchmarks and has served as a solid large-cap bellwether for investors.

## How I Picked 3 Top Dow Jones Stocks

I grabbed tickers of the 30 companies listed on the Dow Jones and ran them through the SA Quant System. I accomplished this by downloading the symbols and pasting them into Seeking Alpha's [Portfolio Analyzer](https://seekingalpha.com/account/portfolio/all/holdings#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/account/portfolio/all/holdings"). I discovered three stocks with solid collective fundamentals: a hyperscaler, an oil giant, and a leading aerospace company.

Seeking Alpha's Quant Ratings are generated by a proprietary model that analyzes more than 100 metrics for each stock relative to sector peers and grades them across five core factors: Valuation, Growth, Profitability, Momentum, and EPS Revisions. Below, I discuss recent context and key drivers of each stock's quant rating.

### 1. Amazon.com, Inc. ([AMZN](https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/AMZN#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "Amazon.com, Inc."))

- Market Capitalization: $2.57T
    
- Quant Rating: Strong Buy
    
- Sector: Consumer Discretionary
    
- Industry: Broadline Retail
    
- Quant Sector Ranking (as of 6/15/2026): 5 out of 478
    
- Quant Industry Ranking (as of 6/15/2026): 1 out of 27
    

Amazon was added to the Dow Jones in 2024 as part of an effort to ensure the index reflected the evolving nature of the economy. Several Dow stocks have long had a deep "Main Street" presence, and Amazon fits the bill, impacting several aspects of consumer life with its wide range of offerings.

Amazon transformed its business model beyond online retail to become a cloud computing giant, one of the largest streaming platforms, and an AI chipmaker. Although Amazon has lagged other AI-focused tech leaders, the stock has soundly outperformed global online retail peers.

- Quant Industry Ranking (as of 6/15/2026): 4 out of 16
    

Chevron's stock has been on a wild ride this year, surging as oil prices topped $100 per barrel amid the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, only to [drop sharply](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4603188-biggest-stock-movers-monday-spcx-crypto-stocks-and-more#source=section%3Amain_content%7Cbutton%3Abody_link "https://seekingalpha.com/news/4603188-biggest-stock-movers-monday-spcx-crypto-stocks-and-more") this week after the U.S. and Iran reportedly struck a new peace deal. Despite the slowdown in price appreciation, Chevron continues to be a solid income stock, offering a safe, consistent, and growing dividend, supported by strong cash flow generation.
