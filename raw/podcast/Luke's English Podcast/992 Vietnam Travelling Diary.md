## 全文摘要

本期是一集纯独白节目。Luke 翻出 2009 年底至 2010 年初独自背包游越南时写下的旅行日记,一边朗读一边即兴补充回忆与语言讲解。行程从胡志明市(西贡)出发,一路向北到河内、下龙湾,再折返顺化、会安,南下芽庄、大叻,最后 32 小时辗转返回伦敦。

旅行本应与表弟 Ollie 同行,但 Ollie 在马拉维 Lake of Stars 音乐节湖中游泳后感染了血吸虫病(bilharzia,他戏称之为 "Bill"),只能退出。于是 Luke 约 75% 的行程都是独自一人,日记里既有对景点(战争遗迹博物馆、高台教寺庙、古芝地道、下龙湾、顺化皇城、会安裁缝铺)的记录,也有大量关于孤独、观察与自我调侃的段子——很多后来都成了他脱口秀的素材。日记结尾他写下新年愿望"想遇到一个聊得来的女孩",而现实中大约五周后他就遇到了后来的妻子。

## 发言总结

### Luke Thompson

以"背包旅行"(backpacking)的定义和 2026 年五月底录音时巴黎的热浪开场,交代日记的由来与独自旅行的心境。随后按时间顺序朗读日记:西贡的物价与文化冲击、战争遗迹博物馆的震撼、高台教与古芝地道;飞往河内后的下龙湾"坑爹船游"、跨年夜的 beer hoi 与卡拉 OK;顺化皇城与"世界尽头"般的荒凉海滩、会安的定制西装与河畔夜色;火车南下芽庄的沙滩与出海游、被银行冻结卡的 ATM 噩梦;最后经大叻返回伦敦。全程穿插对英语学习(English for travelling / nightclubs / presentations)、喜剧演员、电影的评论,以及诚实的孤独感自省,语气自嘲而温暖。

## ~00:00 开场:热浪、背包旅行的定义与 Ollie 的血吸虫病

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> Luke 在 2026 年五月底的热浪(35℃)中录音,解释了"背包旅行"的含义与这份日记的由来。本次越南之旅本应与表弟 Ollie 同行,但 Ollie 在马拉维的音乐节湖中游泳后感染了血吸虫病,只能退出,于是行程变成了约 75% 的独自旅行。

### Luke Thompson @ ~00:00

> Backpacking is a kind of travelling where you carry everything you need on your back, in a backpack, and you tend to travel independently, on a budget, moving from place to place, staying in cheap hotels or hostels.

### Luke Thompson @ ~08:00

> My cousin Ollie was supposed to come with me, but he couldn't because he'd got ill. He'd been swimming in a lake at a festival called Lake of Stars in Malawi, and he'd picked up bilharzia — which is this parasitic worm infection. He called it "Bill". He had to be treated with a zinc pill at the hospital for tropical diseases on Tottenham Court Road.

## ~15:00 西贡:物价、文化冲击与独自旅行的心境

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> 抵达胡志明市(西贡)后,Luke 记下了不到两英镑的胡椒面、满是白人背包客的街道、欣赏的电影,以及独自旅行时对单身生活的自省。他也记录了初到时的文化冲击与过马路要紧跟当地人的小技巧。

### Luke Thompson @ ~15:00

> Pepper noodles for less than two quid. The place was stiff with white backpackers — meaning it was absolutely full of them, packed with them.

### Luke Thompson @ ~22:00

> The trick to crossing the road is to just walk slowly and steadily next to a local, and let the scooters flow around you. Don't stop, don't run — just keep a steady pace.

## ~30:00 战争遗迹博物馆、高台教寺庙与古芝地道

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> Luke 参观了白市场、统一宫与战争遗迹博物馆(展示美军战争罪行,令人震撼),并解释了 grift(小骗局)这个词。随后去了高台教寺庙(佛教+天主教+儒家的融合)和古芝地道(越共游击战术、AK-47 射击场)。

### Luke Thompson @ ~30:00

> The War Remnants Museum was pretty shocking, actually. It documents the war crimes committed during the war, from the Vietnamese perspective, and some of it is really quite hard to look at.

### Luke Thompson @ ~42:00

> A grift is basically a small-scale con or a scam — someone trying to trick you out of a bit of money, usually a tourist. You get grifted, meaning you get scammed in a fairly minor way.

## ~50:00 飞往河内与下龙湾的"坑爹船游"

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> 飞往阴雨的河内后,Luke 参加了一艘"坑爹"的下龙湾船游:床铺就在发动机上方震个不停、Harley-Davidson 发电机、导游收走护照、无救生衣划皮艇、香蕉也被 grift。他因关掉发电机而成了全船英雄。

### Luke Thompson @ ~50:00

> The bed was directly over the engine, so the whole thing vibrated all night. The guide was a total wheeler-dealer — he took everyone's passports, which felt pretty dodgy.

### Luke Thompson @ ~60:00

> We went kayaking with no life jackets, and then there was this dark race back to the boat as the sun went down. I turned off the generator at one point and became a bit of a hero to the other passengers.

## ~70:00 河内跨年夜:beer hoi、pho 与卡拉 OK

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> 回到河内,Luke 住在朋友 Nick 在法式殖民建筑里的住处,记下了 B-52 纪念碑、难喝的炼乳咖啡、咖啡馆里的老鼠。跨年夜他与爱尔兰同事 Ronan 喝 beer hoy、吃咖哩、去 Press Club、唱 Beyoncé MV 卡拉 OK,深夜在 Pho Alley 吃 pho。

### Luke Thompson @ ~70:00

> Vietnamese coffee was terrible — this thick condensed milk at the bottom, like ectoplasm. And there was a rat that ran through the cafe while we were sitting there.

### Luke Thompson @ ~82:00

> New Year's Eve was brilliant. Beer hoy — this really cheap fresh draught beer you drink sitting on tiny plastic stools on the street corner. Then curry, the Press Club, karaoke where we sang along to a Beyoncé video, glitter everywhere, and late-night pho at a place called Pho Alley.

## ~90:00 顺化:皇城、motorbike grift 与"世界尽头"的海滩

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> 凌晨飞抵顺化,Luke 骑自行车逸游安静的皇城,却被摩托车导游 grift。他独自骑车去了 15 公里外一片荒凉、堆着垃圾的"世界尽头"海滩,坐在破塔塔的塔椅上面向南中国海,深刻体会到独自旅行的孤独——也描述了晚上误入一家激光夜总会餐厅的荒诞遗。

### Luke Thompson @ ~90:00

> Finally we reach the beach and it's like the end of the world or something — that's how it felt. I sit there at the edge of the world, staring out across the South China Sea, and I just think to myself: what am I doing here without a girl or, at least, another person?

### Luke Thompson @ ~100:00

> I weaved a web-like tapestry of lies around him, sending him off on a number of mental wild goose chases, ultimately leaving him flummoxed — confused, not knowing what to think — so he gave up the line of questioning.

## ~110:00 会安:定制西装与河畔夜色

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> 乘卧铺巴士到达会安,Luke 走进一家推荐的裁缝店,定制了一黑一白两套西装(白西装是为了像 Steve Martin 那样穿着表演脱口秀,但他从未穿过)。会安是全程最美的地方——河畔餐厅、灯火、轻飘的小船、Bob Dylan 的《Like a Rolling Stone》。

### Luke Thompson @ ~110:00

> I treated myself to two tailored suits — one black, one white. I bought a white suit because I had this idea that I would perform stand-up comedy in a white suit, a bit like Steve Martin did in the 70s. Two hundred dollars for the lot. I never ever wore the white one.

### Luke Thompson @ ~120:00

> I sat by the river listening to Bob Dylan coming from a bar behind me — they were blasting out Like a Rolling Stone, which was a nice atmospheric moment, a special moment. Then I wandered home and chillaxed.

## ~130:00 火车南下芽庄:沙滩、出海游与 ATM 噩梦

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> 从峘港乘火车(四人卧铺、食物糟糕、蟑蟊)南下芽庄。在芽庄海滩遇到缠人的摩托车接客"Bang",与开 TEFL 学校的爸爸聊天。他参加了出海游(水族馆、海龟、浮潜、竹桶鼓手、海上漂浮酒吧),但随后遭遇银行冻结银行卡的 ATM 噩梦。也就是在这一段,他写下了新年愿望"只想遇到一个聊得来的女孩"。

### Luke Thompson @ ~130:00

> The train from Da Nang to Nha Trang was a four-bed cabin with grotty food and cockroaches. I listened to Kings of Convenience the whole way. There was this pushy motorbike guy at the beach who kept saying "Bang! Bang!" trying to get my attention.

### Luke Thompson @ ~142:00

> I wrote down a New Year's resolution: I just want to meet a girl I click with. To click with someone means you connect naturally, you get on really easily. And funnily enough, about five weeks later, I met my wife.

## ~150:00 大叻、32 小时返程与"遇见对的人"

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> Luke 描述了杀价与 MSG 的脱口秀段子、吃腻了越南菜、观察当地人的晨练。最后从大叻(长途巴士刹车冒烟、乏味)经 32 小时辗转——大叻→西贡→多哈→盖特威克→伦敦(降雪)——返回。他列出了"越南印象"清单,以积极的基调收尾,并提到后来又去了柬埔寨、老挝、泰国、印尼。

### Luke Thompson @ ~150:00

> By the end I was completely templed out and foodied out — you know, when you've seen so many temples or eaten so much of the same food that you can't take any more of it.

### Luke Thompson @ ~160:00

> It was a 32-hour journey home: Dalat to Saigon, Saigon to Doha, Doha to Gatwick, and then back to Hammersmith — arriving to snow in London. Looking back, despite all the misadventures, it was a genuinely positive experience.
