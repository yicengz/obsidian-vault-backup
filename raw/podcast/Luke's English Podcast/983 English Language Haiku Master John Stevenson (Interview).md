# 导读 · 精华解析

## 全文摘要

这是一期意外促成的深度访谈。Luke 此前受加拿大听众 Helene 来信启发,做了两期《Haiku in English》节目,朗读了大量 John Stevenson 的俳句;Helene 把节目转给了 John 本人,John 深受触动,主动写信希望与 Luke 对谈。于是有了这场通过 Zoom 录制的对话。

John Stevenson 是英语俳句界数十年的中流砥柱,被视为当今最重要的英语俳句实践者之一,曾任权威期刊《The Heron's Nest》编辑、美国俳句协会主席,著有《Some of the Silence》《Quiet Enough》《My Red》等。

对话前半段,John 澄清并补充了若干俳句的文化背景:芭蕉的青蛙俳句为何是一场"革命"(在此之前,提到青蛙是为唤起蛙鸣,而芭蕉让青蛙发出"扑通"的水声,等于宣告"让我们重新思考俳句");季语(kigo)是形式化而非直觉化的约定(未加修饰的"月"默认指秋月);日本高语境文化与美国低语境文化的差异;以及 5-7-5 音节规则在英语中其实是"翻译中的迷失"。后半段,John 区分了 haiku 与 senryu(川柳):haiku 安静、含蓄、可反复"打开",源自连句(renku)开篇的 jo(序)部;senryu 像笑话,首次冲击力最强,源自中段 ha(破)部——并坦言自己许多作品其实更偏 senryu 或处于两者的边界。

随后 Luke 逐首朗读 John 的俳句,John 回忆每首的创作瞬间与本意:地铁上对着乐谱默动嘴唇的女人、"complicating my love of music, her urge to sing" 的一语双关、"so much of what I do involves my body"、教儿子平行泊车的 5-7-5、"thin winter coat / so little protection against her boyfriend" 的边界诗、裸体海滩上的巨大沙堡等。John 还分享了他的创作哲学(创作由读者完成、要多写"坏俳句"、随身携带记事本与录音笔)、两年前视力受损后"资产-负债"式的人生观,以及由记梦日记练成的清醒梦能力。结尾 Luke 预告了接下来一系列嘉宾节目,并提到第 1000 集将在八月前后到来、播客即将迎来 17 周年。

## 发言总结

### Luke Thompson

Luke 以主持人与"提问的读者"双重身份推进对话。他先请 John 澄清芭蕉青蛙俳句与季语等文化背景,再把话题引向 haiku 与 senryu 的分野,并不断用自己熟悉的"单口喜剧"(stand-up / observational comedy)作类比——setup 与 punchline、留白让观众自行补全,与俳句"由读者完成创作"异曲同工。他坦诚分享在日本生活两年、在酒吧学日语数到 180 的经历,佐证日语的高语境与语序特征。逐首朗读环节,他给出自己的多重解读(如"同款车"一诗白天与夜晚的不同氛围),再听 John 的原意,形成读者与作者的往复。

### John Stevenson

John 博学、坦率而慷慨。他强调俳句的意义高度依赖文化语境,芭蕉之所以伟大在于把原本只在贵族圈流传的艺术改造得贴近新兴市民阶层的现实。他从连句(renku)的 jo-ha-kyu 结构出发界定 haiku(属"序"的安静、含蓄基调)与 senryu(属"破"的放肆、可涉性与争议)的区别,引用编辑 Bob Speece 名言"a haiku might make you smile, a senryu might make you laugh out loud"。谈创作,他主张"多写坏俳句"以免陷入写作障碍,随身带记事本/录音笔捕捉灵感,并认为俳句不追求炫技,而在于与"想象的共同体"分享共同经验、把读者带到某个瞬间后由读者完成创作。他坦然面对两年前的视力受损,提出"任何事都同时是资产与负债"的人生观,并讲述由少年时记梦日记练成的清醒梦——最近还在梦里"重新开车"游历各地。

## ~08:00 芭蕉的青蛙俳句:一场革命

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> John 讲解芭蕉"古池"俳句的革命性:在此之前数百年,提到青蛙是为唤起蛙鸣;芭蕉让青蛙跃入水中发出"扑通"一声,等于宣告"让我们重新思考俳句"。他借"月=秋月"说明季语(kigo)是形式化约定而非直觉。

### John Stevenson @ ~09:00

> "Old pond, a frog jumps in, sound of water... for centuries, the mention of a frog was to invoke the song of the frog. So when Basho presented this frog making a different sound, a splash, this was a very big splash — essentially saying, let us renew our thoughts about haiku."

### John Stevenson @ ~12:00

> "Kigo are not intuitive. Kigo are formal... we have the moon all year long, but the moon in a haiku, if it's mentioned and not modified in any way, is considered to be the autumn moon."

## ~18:00 高低语境与 5-7-5 的迷思

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> John 指出俳句诞生于日本这样高度同质、高语境的文化(少言而意会),与美国这样低语境、需把一切摊在桌面上的文化截然相反。Luke 以在日本生活两年学日语的经历佐证语序与留白差异。两人谈到 5-7-5 音节规则在英语里其实是"翻译中的迷失"。

### John Stevenson @ ~20:00

> "It's a very high context culture... less needs to be said, more unspoken communication — unlike low context cultures, where everything needs to be clearly stated."

### John Stevenson @ ~25:00

> "The idea of trying to base our understanding of haiku on syllable structure is hopeless... Five, seven, five in English is almost always a much longer poem with a lot more information."

## ~28:00 俳句与川柳(senryu)之别

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> John 从连句(renku)的 jo-ha-kyu 结构切入:haiku 承 jo(序)的安静、含蓄、可反复"打开";senryu 承 ha(破)的放肆,像笑话般首次冲击力最强。他坦言自己许多作品其实处于两者边界。

### John Stevenson @ ~30:00

> "The tone of haiku is the tone of the jo. So it's a little bit formal, quieter, subtle. It doesn't involve strong emotion... But once you get into the ha, the party, anything goes. And to me, that's the basis of senryu."

### John Stevenson @ ~34:00

> "Haiku often are so simple and so subtle that on your first reading, they seem like almost nothing... they have this quality of opening and opening and opening. Senryu is really like a joke — it has its maximum impact the first time you hear it." (Bob Speece: "a haiku might make you smile, a senryu might make you laugh out loud.")

## ~40:00 John 如何走上俳句之路

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> John 回忆通过戏剧工作接触俳句:在一次戏剧会议上,一位日本女演员用日语为他吟诵最爱的芭蕉俳句,再逐层讲解其季节、历史、地域与中国典故的文化联想,令他惊叹于"几个字即是多重文化向量的十字路口"。他谈到俳句的"简短"如何要求高度聚焦。

### John Stevenson @ ~42:00

> "She recited the poem for me in Japanese so I could hear the music... then the killer was, for a Japanese person the mention of this season will have this association... it's a crossroads for all these cultural vectors. I was impressed."

### John Stevenson @ ~46:00

> "I wanted to go directly to writing those rather than the whole mass of whatever would surround those."

## ~50:00 创作留白:读者完成艺术

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> John 阐述创作哲学:西方艺术常"if you've got it, flaunt it",追求令观众惊叹的炫技;而俳句相反,让人觉得"我也能写",重在与想象的共同体分享共同经验——诗人把读者带到某个瞬间,由读者完成创作。Luke 以单口喜剧的 setup/punchline 与留白作平行类比。

### John Stevenson @ ~52:00

> "The creative act for haiku doesn't get finished by the haiku poet. The haiku poet, if they're working well, brings the reader to that moment and then the reader finishes the creative act."

### John Stevenson @ ~55:00

> "What I tell people who are just starting with haiku is it's important to write a lot of bad haiku. Because if you tell yourself that I will only do it right, that's a prescription for writer's block."

## ~58:00 逐首解读 John 的俳句

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> Luke 逐首朗读 John 的作品,John 回忆创作瞬间与本意:地铁上对着乐谱默动嘴唇的女人;"complicating my love of music, her urge to sing" 的误导式双关(其实是 my love of her);"so much of what I do involves my body";教儿子平行泊车恰是一首 5-7-5;"thin winter coat / so little protection against her boyfriend" 的边界诗;以及裸体海滩上"his enormous sandcastle"的三重笑点。

### John Stevenson @ ~60:00

> "On the subway, a woman with sheet music moving her lips... I remember where I was sitting and where this other person was sitting. She was hearing something that I couldn't hear."

### John Stevenson @ ~68:00

> "Trying to fit in / time for my son to practise / parallel parking — when you learn to parallel park, it's a three-part process. That 5-7-5 poem to me is a little bit like trying to learn to parallel park."

### Luke Thompson @ ~72:00

> "Thin winter coat, so little protection against her boyfriend... it's a portrait of an unfortunate girl in a toxic relationship. This is why it's a borderline poem — same words, but a different tone."

## ~78:00 清醒梦与睡眠的边界

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> 由"border of sleep, the sound of nearby breathing. Mine."一诗谈起,John 分享面对两年前视力受损的"资产-负债"人生观,以及少年时记梦日记练成的清醒梦——最近还在梦里意识到"我已经在开车了",于是随心游历各地。

### John Stevenson @ ~80:00

> "Everything in life is an asset-liability situation. Anything that's happening is both an asset potentially and a liability... I do mourn the loss of faculties I had before, but I also am enjoying the process of creating new faculties to fill the gaps."

### John Stevenson @ ~83:00

> "I kept a dream journal... eventually I became a lucid dreamer. I would realise I was dreaming while I was dreaming, which would give me the capability of steering the dream... I had a lucid dream where I realised I'm already driving, and I just drove through these great places."
