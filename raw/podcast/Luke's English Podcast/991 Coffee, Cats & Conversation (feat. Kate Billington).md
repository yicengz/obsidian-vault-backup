## 全文摘要

本期 Luke 与老朋友、常驻台湾的英国人 Kate Billington 在台北一家咖啡馆里长谈。Kate 拥有多语言戏剧字幕(surtitling)方向的研究背景——她的硕士论文研究上海戏剧学院与巴黎 Theatre in Paris 的多语言字幕与 AR 字幕眼镜技术。两人当年在巴黎英国文化协会(British Council)相识；如今 Kate 已在台湾生活约 2 年半，在一所法语学校任教。

对话围绕语言学习方法(享受目标语言、别堆砌花哨词汇、有效胜过复杂、语法依然重要)、脱口秀经历(曾为 Mo Magdy 暖场，并用中文完成过一段演出)、她的语言能力(英语母语 / 法语与中文 C2、B1–B2)、专业法式烘焙(自创台湾风味的圣诞肉馅派:芒果、凤梨、红心芭乐、柚子、杨桃、枸杞、洛神花，以及维多利亚时代肉馅派的历史)、猫村(侯硐)带回的猫咪汤匙、印着 \"Guardian reading, tofu eating, Wokerati\" 的马克杯(源自 Suella Braverman 的用语)、台湾与巴黎的对比(治安、公共交通 MRT、人行道骑车、地震)、以及一系列语言学习 App(Skritter 918 天连续打卡、Anki、Quizlet、Duolingo 1201 天连续打卡)。中途还穿插了把英文笑话翻译成中文的\"测试\"环节，以及台北多家咖啡馆(Intro Coffee 等)的推荐——正是在 Intro Coffee 偶遇听众 Tian，才促成了这期节目的重新录制。

## 发言总结

### Luke Thompson

作为主持人，Luke 负责引导话题并不断追问细节:从 Kate 的字幕研究、在台湾的教学生活，到语言学习心得、烘焙与文化差异。他多次把话题拉回\"语言学习\"这条主线，穿插自己对中文之难、对笑话可译性的好奇，并借 Kate 的经历向听众示范如何用英语进行长时段、有深度的自然对话。

### Kate Billington

Kate 分享了自己从多语言戏剧字幕研究者到台湾法语学校教师的路径，以及在台湾近三年的生活观察。她强调语言学习要\"享受过程\"、追求有效沟通而非炫技，并结合脱口秀、烘焙、App 打卡等亲身经历，给出大量可操作的学习与生活建议。

## ~28:00 语言学习方法

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> Kate 的语言学习心得:享受目标语言、不要滥用花哨词汇、有效胜过复杂、语法依然重要。

### Kate Billington @ ~28:30

> My biggest piece of advice is: enjoy the language. If you're not enjoying it, you won't stick with it. And don't try to use fancy, complicated words just to sound impressive — being effective is far better than being complicated.

### Kate Billington @ ~33:00

> People sometimes think grammar doesn't matter anymore, but it really does. It's the framework that lets you actually be understood. You can have all the vocabulary in the world, but without structure it falls apart.

## ~52:00 法式烘焙与台式肉馅派

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> Kate 是受过专业训练的法式烘焙师，自创了一系列台湾风味的圣诞肉馅派，并谈到维多利亚时代肉馅派的历史。

### Kate Billington @ ~52:30

> I trained in French patisserie, so baking is a big part of my life. This year I invented a range of Taiwanese mince pies — using mango, pineapple, red guava, pomelo, star fruit, goji berries and roselle. A little fusion of a British Christmas tradition and Taiwanese flavours.

### Kate Billington @ ~56:00

> The funny thing is, if you go back to Victorian times, mince pies actually did contain meat — real minced beef alongside the fruit and spices. So in a way, playing with the recipe is just continuing a very long tradition of reinvention.

## ~64:00 Wokerati 马克杯

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> Kate 带了一个印着 \"Guardian reading, tofu eating, Wokerati\" 的马克杯——这句话源自英国政治人物 Suella Braverman 的用语，后来被自嘴地当成了身份标签。

### Kate Billington @ ~64:30

> I've got this mug that says \"Guardian reading, tofu eating, Wokerati\". It comes from a phrase Suella Braverman used — she meant it as an insult, but a lot of people just went, well, yes, that's me, and wore it as a badge of honour.

### Luke Thompson @ ~67:00

> That's very British, isn't it — taking an insult from a politician and turning it into a slogan on a mug. Reclaiming the language completely.

## ~70:00 台湾与巴黎的对比

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> Kate 对比台湾与巴黎的生活:台湾更安全、摩擦更少，并用 Mary Norton 1952 年的小说《借物小人》(The Borrowers)开了个玩笑。

### Kate Billington @ ~70:30

> Taiwan feels incredibly safe compared to Paris. You can leave your phone on a cafe table and it'll still be there when you come back. In Paris you'd never dream of that — the \"borrowers\" would have it in seconds.

### Kate Billington @ ~74:00

> There's just so much less friction to daily life here. Everything works, people are considerate, and you don't spend energy being on your guard all the time the way you sometimes do in a big European city.

## ~78:00 交通与地震

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要

### Kate Billington @ ~78:30

> The MRT here is fantastic — clean, punctual, everyone queues properly. Although people do cycle on the pavement, which took me a while to get used to. You learn to keep your wits about you as a pedestrian.

### Kate Billington @ ~82:00

## ~84:00 语言学习 App

[[#章节速览|↑ 返回章节速览]]

> [!QUOTE] 章节概要
> Kate 推荐一系列语言学习 App:Skritter(918 天连续打卡、笔顺与间隔重复)、Anki、Quizlet、Duolingo(1201 天连续打卡)。

### Kate Billington @ ~84:30

> For Chinese characters, Skritter is my absolute favourite — I'd rate it ten out of ten. It teaches you stroke order and uses spaced repetition. My streak is 918 days, which is basically exactly how long I've been in Taiwan.

### Kate Billington @ ~88:00

> I also use Anki and Quizlet for vocabulary, and Duolingo just for the fun of it — my streak there is over 1200 days. You get weirdly attached to keeping the owl, Duo, happy. I even do the chess on there now.

### Luke Thompson @ ~90:30

### Kate Billington @ ~93:00
